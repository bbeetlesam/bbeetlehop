# Bbeetlehop

A beetle loves to lands on something seemingly aimless, but sometimes it has a purpose!
Now, what if your screen is its perch? What kind of beetle who does that? It's **bbeetlehop**!

> Bbeetlehop is a _beetle_ that hoops around your screen, with a single reason: to make your
> worklife easier.

**Bbeetlehop** is a [Quickshell](https://quickshell.org/) configuration that
renders a single Wayland **layer-shell desktop widget**: a small card that waits just below
the bottom edge of the screen and slides up when you call it. It is a thin QML layer
that sits directly on the compositor, stays out of the way, and only accepts interactions
inside the card itself.

Think of it more like Windows' Control Panel, born for a Linux desktop: one small
surface meant to hold settings, toggles, and everyday controls, instead of making you open a
separate window for each one.

The beetle is small, the code is small, and the goal is the same: show a little, help a
little, then leave your desktop alone.
